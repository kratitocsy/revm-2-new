The problem

02 · Problem

Exam-prep students are stuck between two broken options.

Study alone against a phone built to distract you — or lean on productivity apps that just replace one manipulative reward loop with another.

Distraction is the default

Phones and browsers are engineered for engagement, not focus — the environment actively works against the thing you're trying to do.

Willpower alone doesn't scale

Blockers with a one-tap exit get disabled the moment they're inconvenient — friction has to be designed in, not assumed.

Studying is isolating

Serious aspirants often prep alone or in expensive coaching hubs, missing the peer accountability that keeps consistent habits alive.

Existing apps copy dark patterns

Streaks-for-guilt, variable rewards, engineered urgency — the same playbook students are trying to escape.
