About Wynko

Focus, without manipulation.

Wynko is a live study and social platform for competitive-exam aspirants — JEE, NEET, CAT, UPSC and beyond. Millions of students prep alone against a phone built to distract them, or lean on productivity apps that just swap one manipulative reward loop for another.

Wynko is built around autonomy, competence, and genuine peer connection instead — every gamified mechanic is reviewed against that principle before it ships.
