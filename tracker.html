Memory Strength — est., based on the forgetting curve
Forgetting Curve
How to read this: the curve is Hermann Ebbinghaus's classic retention model — recall starts at 100% right after you study a topic and decays exponentially the longer you go without revisiting it. Each gold spike marks a review you actually completed on time, which resets retention back up — exactly the "spaced repetition" effect the 5min/12h/Day1…Day30 schedule is built around. A red dashed stretch means a review was missed, so the curve keeps fading uninterrupted instead of bouncing back — that's the visual cost of skipping a scheduled revision. The steeper and longer the unbroken decay, the more of that topic is realistically slipping out of memory. This is a simplified approximation (exponential decay, τ≈6 days) for motivation and prioritization, not a clinical measure of your actual recall.
