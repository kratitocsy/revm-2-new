Double-click the .zip to unzip it. Keep the resulting folder somewhere permanent (like Documents) — don't delete it later, Chrome reads the extension straight from it.

chrome://extensions

Copy chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions if you use Edge) into your browser's address bar at the top and press Enter.

4Click "Load unpacked"

Three new buttons appear once Developer mode is on — click Load unpacked, then pick the unzipped revm2-extension folder from Step 1.

Off
On

Required, not optional. On the RevM² card on this page, click Details, scroll down, and turn on Allow in Incognito. Without this, RevM² can't run in a Private/Incognito window at all, and the desktop app will keep closing your browser to force the point — even during completely normal, non-private browsing.
