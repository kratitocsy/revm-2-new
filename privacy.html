Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

RevM² ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Account data: When you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and name from Google OAuth.
  • Usage data: Your revision tracker entries, study timer sessions, and onboarding preferences are stored to provide the service.
  • Analytics: We use Google Analytics to understand how the app is used. This collects anonymised usage data.
  • Advertising: We use Google AdSense to display ads. Google may use cookies to show relevant ads based on your browsing history.

How we use your information

  • To provide and improve the RevM² service
  • To personalise your tracker and dashboard
  • To improve percentile predictions using anonymised score submissions
  • To display relevant advertisements through Google AdSense

Data storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (PostgreSQL). Tracker data is also stored locally in your browser's localStorage for offline access.

Third-party services

  • Google OAuth — for sign-in
  • Supabase — for database and authentication
  • Google Analytics — for usage analytics
  • Google AdSense — for advertisements

Your rights

You may request deletion of your account and data at any time by contacting us. Score submissions are stored anonymously and cannot be individually identified or deleted.

Cookies

We use cookies for authentication and advertising. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect app functionality.

Contact

For any privacy concerns, contact us at: privacy@revm2.in

This privacy policy applies to revm2.vercel.app and revm2.in.
